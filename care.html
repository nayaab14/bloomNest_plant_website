SIMPLE TIPS, HAPPIER PLANTS

Plant care, made easy.

You do not need a green thumb to get started. Begin with these simple habits.

☀

Find the right light

Check whether your plant likes bright indirect light, direct sun, or a shadier corner. Move it gradually if needed.

💧

Water thoughtfully

Check the soil before watering. Many houseplants prefer the top layer of soil to dry out between waterings.

🪴

Choose a good pot

Use a pot with drainage holes so extra water can escape and roots are less likely to stay soggy.

🌿

Keep leaves clean

Wipe dusty leaves gently with a soft, damp cloth so they look fresh.

🌱

Give plants space

Avoid placing plants too close together. Good airflow helps keep leaves healthy.

♡

Watch and learn

Notice changes in leaves and soil. Each plant is different, so adjust your routine as you learn.

Beginner tip

More water is not always better. Check the soil and the needs of your specific plant before watering.
